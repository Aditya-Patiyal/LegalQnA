Privacy

What we store: your account email, uploaded documents, extracted text, chat history, and generated documents — each tied to your account and readable only by you (enforced by database row-level security, not just application code).

What we send to third parties: to answer a question or analyze a document, relevant text is sent to Groq (our AI provider) to generate a response, and to Hugging Face's inference API to generate search embeddings. Neither is used to train models on your data beyond that single request.

What we don't do: we don't sell your data, share it with other users, or use it for advertising.

Deleting your data: delete any document from your dashboard to remove it, its extracted text, and its search index immediately. Deleting your account removes everything tied to it.
