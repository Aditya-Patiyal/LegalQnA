What actually happens to your data

No vague promises — here's the real data flow

Your documents are processed to answer your questions

Uploaded files are stored in your private, account-scoped storage. To answer a question, the relevant excerpts are sent to our AI provider (Groq) to generate a response — the same way any AI-assisted tool works. Nothing is shared publicly or used to train models.

Real per-account isolation

Access is enforced by database-level row-level security, not just application logic — your documents, chat history, and generated files are only ever queryable by you.

You can delete your data

Delete any uploaded document from your dashboard at any time — it's removed from storage and search immediately.
